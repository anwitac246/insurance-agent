# Comprehensive Auto Insurance - Depreciation Rules

## Parts Depreciation
When replacing vehicle parts during a repair, SafeGuard Auto Insurance applies the following standard depreciation rates based on the material of the part:

* **Rubber, Nylon, and Plastic Parts**: 50% depreciation.
* **Glass Parts**: 0% depreciation (fully covered).
* **Fiberglass Components**: 30% depreciation.
* **Wooden Parts**: 5% depreciation for the first year, increasing by 5% each subsequent year up to 50%.

## Age-Based Depreciation for Metal Parts
For all other parts (primarily metal components), depreciation is calculated based on the age of the vehicle from the date of initial registration:

* **0 to 6 months**: Nil (0%)
* **6 months to 1 year**: 5%
* **1 year to 2 years**: 10%
* **2 years to 3 years**: 15%
* **3 years to 4 years**: 25%
* **4 years to 5 years**: 35%
* **5 years to 10 years**: 40%
* **Over 10 years**: 50%

## Zero Depreciation Add-on
If the policyholder has purchased the "Zero Depreciation" or "Bumper-to-Bumper" add-on rider, the above depreciation rates do not apply, and parts are replaced at 100% cost (subject to standard deductibles and limited to a maximum of two claims per policy year).
