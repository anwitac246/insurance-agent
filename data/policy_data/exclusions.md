# Comprehensive Auto Insurance - Exclusions

## General Exclusions
SafeGuard Auto Insurance will not provide coverage or pay for claims arising under the following circumstances.

## Unauthorized Drivers
* **Unlicensed Drivers**: No coverage if the vehicle is operated by a driver without a valid driver's license.
* **Suspended License**: No coverage if the driver's license was suspended or revoked at the time of the incident.
* **Excluded Drivers**: No coverage if the driver is specifically excluded on the policy declarations page.

## Illegal Activities
* **Intoxication**: No coverage for damages occurring while the driver is under the influence of alcohol (BAC above legal limit) or illegal drugs.
* **Fleeing Law Enforcement**: No coverage for damages sustained while attempting to evade arrest or law enforcement.

## Vehicle Usage
* **Commercial Use**: Standard personal auto policies do not cover vehicles used for commercial purposes (e.g., delivery services, ride-sharing like Uber/Lyft) unless a specific commercial rider is attached.
* **Racing**: No coverage for damages occurring during organized racing, speed contests, or on a designated race track.
* **Intentional Damage**: No coverage for damages intentionally caused by the policyholder.
