# Comprehensive Auto Insurance - Claims Procedure

## Initial Reporting
* All accidents must be reported to SafeGuard Auto Insurance within 48 hours of the incident.
* Theft or vandalism must be reported to the police within 24 hours.

## Mandatory Documents
To process a claim, the following documents are strictly required:
1. **First Information Report (FIR)**: Mandatory for theft, vandalism, hit-and-run, or major accidents involving third-party injuries. Not required for minor single-vehicle dings.
2. **Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC)**: Valid RC of the insured vehicle.
3. **Driving License**: Valid driving license of the person operating the vehicle at the time of the incident.
4. **Repair Estimate**: A detailed quote from an authorized or network garage.

## Photographic Evidence
* **Damage Photos**: A minimum of 4 clear photographs showing the vehicle damage from different angles.
* **Scene Photos**: Photographs of the accident scene and any third-party vehicles involved (if applicable and safe to capture).

## Assessment
* A certified SafeGuard Surveyor must inspect the vehicle before any repair work commences.
* Reimbursement is strictly based on the surveyor's approved amount, subject to policy deductibles and depreciation rules.
