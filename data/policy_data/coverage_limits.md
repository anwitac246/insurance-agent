# Comprehensive Auto Insurance - Coverage Limits

## Policy Overview
This document outlines the standard coverage limits for the SafeGuard Auto Insurance Comprehensive Policy.

## Collision Coverage
* **Maximum Payout**: Up to the Actual Cash Value (ACV) of the vehicle at the time of the incident.
* **Standard Deductible**: $500 per incident.
* **Covered Events**: Collisions with other vehicles, single-car roll-overs, collisions with stationary objects (trees, fences, poles).

## Comprehensive Coverage
* **Maximum Payout**: Up to the Actual Cash Value (ACV) of the vehicle at the time of the incident.
* **Standard Deductible**: $250 per incident.
* **Covered Events**: Theft, vandalism, fire, natural disasters (hail, floods, hurricanes), falling objects, and animal collisions (e.g., hitting a deer).

## Liability Coverage
* **Bodily Injury**: $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident.
* **Property Damage**: $50,000 per accident.
* **Covered Events**: Legal fees, medical bills, and repair costs for third parties when the policyholder is at fault.
