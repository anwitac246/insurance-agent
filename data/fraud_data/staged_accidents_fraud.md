# Fraud Case Studies - Staged Accidents

## Case 301: The "Swoop and Squat"
* **Fraud Type**: Organized Staged Collision
* **Scenario**: Claimant reported rear-ending a vehicle that "braked suddenly for a dog." The third party in the front vehicle claimed severe whiplash.
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - Telematics data from the policyholder's car showed no sudden braking by the front vehicle until immediately after a third vehicle (the "swoop" car) aggressively cut them off and sped away.
  - The occupants of the front vehicle had a history of 4 similar claims in the past 2 years with different insurers.
* **Resolution**: Claim denied. Referred to the organized fraud investigation unit.

## Case 302: The Phantom Vehicle Hit-and-Run
* **Fraud Type**: Fabricated Incident to Cover Self-Inflicted Damage
* **Scenario**: Claimant alleged their parked car was sideswiped overnight by an unknown vehicle.
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - The scratch patterns were horizontal, but contained traces of yellow concrete paint, not automotive paint.
  - An investigator matched the paint transfer and scrape height to a concrete pillar in the claimant's own apartment parking garage.
* **Resolution**: Claim denied as the incident was misrepresented. (Would have been covered under comprehensive, but denied due to lying on the official claim form).

## Case 303: The "Jump-In" Passenger
* **Fraud Type**: Fictitious Injuries
* **Scenario**: Following a legitimate low-speed fender bender, three people filed for bodily injury compensation.
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - The first responder's report and dashcam footage from the other driver clearly showed only one occupant (the driver) in the claimant's vehicle at the time of the crash.
  - The two "passengers" arrived at the scene 15 minutes later.
* **Resolution**: Property damage approved. Bodily injury claims for the two jump-in passengers aggressively denied and reported for insurance fraud.

## Case 304: The Paper Collision
* **Fraud Type**: Completely Fabricated Accident
* **Scenario**: Two parties submit perfectly matching damage reports, police reports, and repair estimates claiming they collided at an intersection. 
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - A physical inspection of both vehicles showed existing rust inside the scratches.
  - Paint transfer analysis (spectroscopy) showed the paint on Car A did not match the chemical makeup of Car B's factory paint, despite them claiming they hit each other. 
* **Resolution**: Claims denied. Both parties referred for collusion fraud.

## Case 305: The Directed Pedestrian
* **Fraud Type**: Staged Pedestrian Strike
* **Scenario**: A pedestrian claims severe injuries after allegedly being hit by the policyholder at a slow-speed crosswalk. 
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - Nearby store security camera footage shows the pedestrian waiting at the crosswalk, letting 3 cars pass, and then intentionally throwing themselves onto the hood of the policyholder's slow-moving car.
  - The pedestrian had knee pads under their jeans to brace for the fall.
* **Resolution**: Liability claim denied. Pedestrian reported to police.

## Case 306: The "Helpful" Tow Truck
* **Fraud Type**: Post-Accident Deliberate Damage
* **Scenario**: Following a minor fender bender, a rogue tow truck arrives immediately and tows the car to a specific shady garage.
* **Detection Indicators**:
  - The police photos at the scene show minor bumper damage. 
  - The photos submitted by the garage the next day show the entire front axle ripped out and the radiator smashed. 
  - Grease marks matching the tow hook were found on the deliberately broken axle.
* **Resolution**: Claim approved only for the initial bumper damage. Garage flagged and blacklisted.
